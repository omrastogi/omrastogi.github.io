I am an MS student in Artificial Intelligence at [Northeastern University's Khoury College of Computer Sciences](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/) (Sep 2025–Aug 2027), affiliated with the [Augmented Cognition Lab](https://ostadabbas.sites.northeastern.edu/) under [Prof. Sarah Ostadabbas](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/ostadabbas-sarah/). My research spans multimodal large language models, computer vision, diffusion-based generative models, and 3D/depth estimation.

Current work includes **AdShot**, a benchmark evaluating MLLMs on advertisement video clipping (30s → 15s) across 823 video pairs and 194 brands — currently under review at IJCAI 2026 — and a multi-person extension of GVHMR using scene detection, Visual Odometry patching, SMPL mesh overlay, and YOLO tracking, supporting an IARPA MOVES pre-proposal. Prior to Northeastern, I spent 14 months at the [Vision and AI Lab at IISc](https://val.cds.iisc.ac.in/) developing DepthDiT, a Diffusion Transformer for monocular depth estimation (best checkpoint: AbsRel 0.107, δ1 0.88), and 6 months at [Dashtoon](https://dashtoon.com/) shipping production diffusion pipelines for animated character generation including Flux LoRA adapters across 20+ characters and Hidream-l1 MoE integration.

I am actively seeking **Summer 2026 research and engineering internships** in computer vision, multimodal ML, and applied AI research.
